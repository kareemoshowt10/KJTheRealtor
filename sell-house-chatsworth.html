What is the house being asked to do?

When selling is the honest move

The next chapter needs cash. The carrying cost is the problem. The house no longer fits the people in it. A clean sale, priced from real comps and prepared without a renovation fantasy, is the right instrument.

When keeping wins

The lot can hold an ADU. Horses or RV storage already rent. The tax basis is old and Prop 19 makes a transfer delicate. Selling that package to chase a portal number is how families give away the only scarce thing they own.

What you get before a sign exists.

A pocket-level value, a rent range that respects the actual parcel, and a plain statement of what I would do if it were my family’s house.
