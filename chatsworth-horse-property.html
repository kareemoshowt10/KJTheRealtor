Foothill and north

The culture is the rocks. The rights are the code.

Stoney Point and the old ranch land explain why Chatsworth still feels like horse country. The municipal code explains whether your lot is allowed to participate. Those are not the same document.

What the premium buys: land, a use, and sometimes a barn older than the kitchen. It does not automatically buy turnkey stalls or a neighbor who wants horses next door.

If you already own one, do not sell it on a tract-home comp. Run the hold math before the sign. I grew up with this landscape. I still will not guess.
