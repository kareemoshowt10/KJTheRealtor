# The 91311 Daily Operating Framework

Everything in the Market Entry Playbook — the content, the farming, the listing intake system — runs *on top of* this. This is the daily discipline underneath it. Three questions, answered every day, in this order:

1. **The ONE Thing** — what's the single action today that makes everything else easier or unnecessary?
2. **Relationships** — who did you nurture today?
3. **Income** — what did you do today/this week that's directly tied to money coming in?

Log lives in Notion: **[91311 Daily Operating Log](https://app.notion.com/p/0ff010e28c814b2f8cb2bf367823fb06)**. A fresh check-in fires every morning at 7:00 AM Pacific (push + email) — see "The daily trigger" below.

---

## 1. The ONE Thing

> "What's the ONE Thing I can do today such that by doing it, everything else becomes easier or unnecessary?" — the Gary Keller framing, applied to 91311.

This isn't a to-do list. It's the single domino that, if you knock it down first, makes the rest of the day's tasks smaller or moot. Most days it will be one of:

- A conversation with a specific stale-listing owner or their agent (see the listing intake system's "repositioning needed" list)
- A piece of content that goes out *today*, not "this week"
- A follow-up call on a live deal that's stalling
- A sphere touch that's been sitting undone for a week

**The test:** if you only did this one thing today and nothing else, would the day still count as a win? If yes, that's the ONE Thing. If you can't answer that in under 30 seconds each morning, that's the sign the list is too long — cut it back to one.

**What it is not:** admin, inbox-zero, social scrolling framed as "content research," or anything that feels productive but doesn't move a specific deal, relationship, or piece of authority-building forward.

---

## 2. Relationships (nurtured daily, not campaigned quarterly)

Every real estate business is a relationship business wearing a marketing costume. The playbook's farming/content cadence assumes this layer is happening underneath it every single day — not just on the weeks you remember to do a mailer.

**Daily minimum:** at least one real touch with a name attached — not a broadcast post. A text, a call, a DM reply, a doorstep hello, a "thinking of you" to a past client or sphere contact.

**What counts:**
- A former client check-in (not asking for anything — just checking in)
- A reply to someone who engaged with a Chatsworth post
- A note to the owner or agent of a stale 91311 listing
- Introducing yourself to one new person in the zip (equestrian business, trail regular, neighbor)

**What doesn't count as the day's relationship touch:** posting content. Content earns reach; it doesn't nurture a specific relationship. Both matter — they're tracked separately.

**Log it as:** name(s) + method. "Called the Reyes family, checking in 6 months post-close" is a real log entry. "Posted on IG" is not.

---

## 3. Income (today or this week)

The blunt question: what did you do that's actually tied to money, not just visibility?

**What counts:**
- An appointment set (buyer consult, listing appointment, showing)
- Contract activity (offer written, counter, an escrow milestone you pushed forward)
- A qualified lead converted to a real next step (not just a form fill)
- Direct follow-up on a live deal that's at risk of stalling

**What doesn't count:** publishing content, admin, or research — those feed the pipeline but aren't the pipeline itself. It's fine for a day's honest answer to be "none logged, this was a foundation day" — the point is naming it truthfully, not padding it.

**Weekly rollup:** every log entry carries a "Week Of" so you can look back on Sunday and ask the real question — did this week produce income-generating activity, or did it produce busywork that felt like it?

---

## How the three pillars interact

They're ordered on purpose. The ONE Thing often *is* a relationship touch or an income activity — when it is, you're not doing three separate things, you're doing one thing well. The framework isn't three chores; it's a lens for picking the one action each day that counts on more than one axis.

If a day's ONE Thing has nothing to do with either relationships or income, ask whether it's actually the highest-leverage thing available, or just the most urgent-feeling one.
