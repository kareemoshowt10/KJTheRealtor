Where it still exists

Foothill and northern pockets, against the rocks and the pass — not every street that can see a hill. Stoney Point and the old ranch land explain the culture. The municipal code explains the rights.

What the premium buys

Land, a use, and sometimes a barn that is older than the kitchen. It does not automatically buy turnkey stalls, trailer access, or a neighbor who wants horses next door.

If you already own one

Do not sell it on a tract-home comp. Horse facilities, RV storage, and acreage rent and resell on a different sheet. Run the hold math before the sign.

A short list I will not skip.

Animal-keeping rules for that parcel. Whether the improvements were permitted. How a trailer actually gets in and out. Water, shade, and what the next buyer will argue about. If any of those fail, the listing is a house with a rural filter on the photos.
